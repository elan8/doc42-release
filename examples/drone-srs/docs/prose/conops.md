The drone is operated by a single pilot from a ground control station (GCS). The pilot plans a
mission, launches the drone, and monitors the live video stream for the duration of the flight.
The flight controller keeps the drone within its geofence and returns it to the launch point when
the command-and-control link is lost or the battery runs low.

The drone supports these operational scenarios:

1. **Patrol and overwatch** along a predefined or manual route, with continuous video to the GCS.
2. **Point-of-interest orbit**: circling a fixed point with the camera on its centre.
3. **Follow target**: tracking a moving object with the gimbal while flying safely.
4. **Inspection** along a structured path, capturing high-resolution stills and video.
5. **Search** of an area with the thermal camera, loitering and zooming as needed.

Each scenario ends with an automated or assisted *return to launch* and landing.
